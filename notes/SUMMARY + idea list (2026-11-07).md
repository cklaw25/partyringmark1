## Ideas
### incident.io: live agent with personality
1. Courtside: an AI basketball commentator watching a camera (paper-ball-into-bin demo).
2. Hype Man: listens to your pitch live, cheers, heckles your "um"s, and coaches your pacing. Demo it DURING the real pitch.
3. Phone Goblin: an ESP32-CAM desk creature that watches you study. The servo head snaps round and nags when you pick up your phone.
4. Backseat: watches your game screen and roasts or coaches you live.

### Brevan Howard: real discovery vs luck
1. The Skeptic: proposes patterns, then tries to break them (unseen data, 1000x shuffle test, penalty for trying many ideas). Feed it random noise and it rejects everything.
2. Hot Hand Court: a courtroom game. A prosecutor AI and a defence AI argue whether a sports streak is real, the stats are the evidence, and the court gives a verdict.
3. Skill or Luck?: give it a finfluencer's or trader's track record. It simulates 10,000 random "monkey" traders and shows where this person ranks.
4. Miracle Cure Check: health or A/B-test claims. Shows how "peeking" and many tries create fake wins.

### Marshall Wace: New Worlds
1. Far Worlds: fly to real exoplanets, with each world generated from real NASA data and physics.
2. First Contact: two AI civilisations on a simulated planet invent their own language and trade, and you watch it emerge.
3. Sound Worlds: any song or voice becomes a planet you can walk on (audio -> terrain).
4. Market Galaxy: stocks are planets, correlation is gravity, and you discover hidden clusters.

### Capital One: Make Credit Accessible (regular people, independently)
1. Day Zero: a swipe-card life sim, starting the UK credit system from zero (students, newcomers, first card). Your own credit-score engine.
2. Can I Afford It?: one question before you buy. It checks your budget, upcoming bills and credit use, then gives yes/no + why. Voice or one button.
3. Budget Boss Fight: your monthly budget is a game boss, and real (mock) transactions are the attacks.
4. Statement Translator: photo of a confusing card statement or letter -> plain words + "what to do next" (minimum payment trap, APR).

## Top picks

2. The Skeptic / Skill or Luck? (Brevan Howard)
3. Day Zero or Can I Afford It? (Capital One)
