# Judge / mentor feedback log

## Opener (30 s)
"Hi, I'm Memphis from team ___. We're on the incident.io track. Can I get 2 minutes of your honest opinion? We're deciding whether to double down or change direction."
Then the 1-line pitch: "THE VAULT is a horror maze where the enemy AI watches how you play, remembers it, and rewires the maze against you. You can also trick it."

## Questions (ask in this order)
1. "From that one line, what do you think it does?" (tests whether the pitch is clear)
2. "On a scale of 1-10, how well does it fit your prompt? What would make it a 10?"
3. "What's the most interesting part to you? And what sounds weakest or most gimmicky?"
4. "If you only saw 2 minutes of the demo, what would you need to see to be impressed?"
5. "Have you seen anything similar today? What would make ours stand out?"
6. (if technical) "We split it into a fast code path and a slower AI strategist. Does that sound right, or would you do it differently?"
Close: "Can I come back with a demo around 9pm / tomorrow morning?" + get their name.

## Decision rule
- STICK if most say fit >= 7 and they get excited about the same part (e.g. maze changes / deception).
- TWEAK if fit 5-7 or they ask for one missing thing (e.g. "more personality", "use the camera") -> add it, keep the core.
- RETHINK if fit < 5 from 2+ judges or nobody can repeat back what it does.
Weigh incident.io judges > organisers > other sponsors.
