## How judging works
Each gold sponsor sets a problem statement, revealed at the opening. Your team picks **one**, submits on Devpost with plenty of images, and then gives a **5-minute live pitch to that sponsor**. A video isn't required; the live demo is what gets judged. Submissions close **11:00 Sunday**. Sponsor reps judge their own tracks. The WHACK lead organisers are also listed as judges, and they're probably the ones deciding the side prizes. No individual judges are named anywhere public.

## Ways to win
1. **A gold track:** about £1k plus 4 gadgets.
2. **Most Creative / WHACKiest (£600 each):** your track project can win these too. Last year's Most Creative was an AR Monopoly game on a 3D-printed board.
3. **1 Hour Hackathon at 22:30:** a free extra shot. One teammate can do it while the rest keep building.
4. **Best Costume:** just dress up. In 2024 the best-dressed team also won a track.

## Tips
- **Bring hardware** (ESP32, servos, sensors). Almost nobody does, and it has won both Marshall Wace and Most Creative before.
- **Capital One angle nobody has used:** credit-building for people new to the UK credit system. That's who Capital One UK actually serves (people with little or no credit history), and it's your own story as an international student.
- **Video:** use Suli's tool or Higgsfield to make a 20–30 second clip for Devpost and as a backup if the live demo breaks. Spend 30 minutes on it at most, on Sunday morning.
- **Careers:** the sponsor talks run 13:00–16:00 and speed networking is at 17:00. The people you meet there are probably your judges. Bring your quant CV. The file has questions to ask each sponsor.
