    THE LIVING LABYRINTH

    In most games, you learn the maze. In this one, the maze learns you.

    The labyrinth is controlled by THE HOST. It watches how you move, remembers it, and rewires the maze against you.

    Goal: find the fuse → restore power at the generator → take the keycard from security → reach the exit.

    You can trick it. Throw bottles. Fake a favourite route. But it learns.
